❓ TROUBLESHOOTING

Q: Script doesn't find Windows Terminal?
A: That's OK! Falls back to VSCode or manual setup. Both work fine.

Q: Port-forwards show "Connection refused"?
A: Make sure Kubernetes pods are running:
   kubectl get pods -n ryzanstein-staging

Q: Dashboard shows no data?
A: Give it 30 seconds for Prometheus to scrape metrics.
   Or manually curl the API: curl http://localhost:8000/health

Q: Terminals keep closing?
A: The -NoExit flag should keep them open. If not, re-run START_PHASE6_NOW.bat
